What it does

A wallet first. A yield engine second.

Send, receive, hold — same as any XRP wallet. The difference only shows up when you want it to.

01

Enable yield in one tap

Move any amount of your balance into the yield reserve. Flax routes 70% into FXRP and deploys it to the FlaxYieldVault strategy contract on Flare; 30% stays as a liquid buffer under the same position.

＋Position tracked on-chain, keyed to your XRPL address
＋Every deposit produces a verifiable custody + mint + stake receipt
＋No lockups — add or withdraw whenever
02

Spend it like it was never staked

Send a payment larger than your liquid balance and Flax settles the gap from your yield reserve automatically. The buffer pays instantly; the vault rebalances by unstaking FXRP behind it. No waiting on an unstake window.

＋One balance, one send flow — yielded or not
＋Rebalance transaction linked in every receipt
＋Withdraw the rest anytime, yield included
Under the hood

Where the yield actually comes from

Flare Confidential Compute holds custody inside a TEE, splits deposits, and rebalances on demand — so your wallet only ever has to show one number.
